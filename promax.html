Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and succeeding in PRO Max Classes.

Who is eligible for PRO Max Classes?

PRO Max Classes are open to enrolled students from Grade III upwards who show an aptitude for deeper academic exploration and competitive challenges. Selection is based on an internal diagnostic screening test and teacher recommendations.

Does PRO Max interfere with regular CBSE board studies?

Not at all. In fact, PRO Max reinforces CBSE textbook mastery by teaching students the underlying 'why' and 'how' of every concept, resulting in consistently superior board examination scores.

Are study materials and mock tests provided?

Yes. Students receive curated problem-solving modules, past years' competitive papers, workbook assignments, and regular computer-based / OMR-based simulated test sessions.

How do parents track student progress?

Periodic analytical reviews and mentor consultations are conducted to discuss each child’s benchmark scores, accuracy ratios, and recommended practice drills.
