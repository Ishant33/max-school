School Policy

4. Refund & Withdrawal Policy

At MAX International School, Assandh, fee refunds and student withdrawals are governed by transparent, structured guidelines to ensure fair administrative processing.

New Admission Cancellation (Prior to Attendance)

Prior to Attendance

If a newly admitted student withdraws due to unavoidable circumstances before attending a single day of school:

  • Eligible Refund: 100% of Caution Money, Tuition Fees, Annual Charges, and Transport/Mess Service Charges (if paid) will be refunded upon receipt of a written cancellation application.
  • Condition: Applies strictly if the student has not attended school for even a single day in the new academic session.
  • Non-Refundable: Basic administrative/registration application processing fees.

Mid-Term Withdrawals

Mid-Term

If a student is withdrawn during the ongoing academic session, the following rules apply:

  • Written Notice: Parents must submit a written withdrawal application to the Principal well in advance.
  • Tuition & Transport Charges: Parents must pay full monthly Tuition and Transport Service Charges for the month in which the child is withdrawn. A fraction/part of a month is calculated as a full month.
  • Proportionate Refund: Any advance tuition fees paid beyond the month of withdrawal will be refunded.
  • Annual Charges: Annual charges are calculated on a quarterly basis. Parents must pay annual charges up to the quarter in which the child is withdrawn; proportionate annual charges deposited in advance for subsequent quarters will be refunded.

Withdrawal at the Start of a New Session

New Session
  • Notice Deadline: Written notification must be submitted on or before the first day of the new academic session.
  • Late Notice Penalty: If notice is given after the first day, mid-term withdrawal rules apply (full month's Tuition, Transport, and Mess charges for the current month will be charged, and advance quarter fees/annual charges will be refunded proportionately).

Caution Money Refund Rules

Caution Money
  • Processing Timeline: Caution money is processed for refund 6 months after the date of issue of the School Leaving Certificate (TC).
  • Claim Deadline: Parents must submit a written claim application for caution money within 6 months from the TC issuance date. Unclaimed caution money beyond 6 months will be forfeited and rights to claim stand relinquished.
  • Payment Mode: Refund cheques are drawn strictly in the name of the parent/guardian who signed the original enrollment form.

School Leaving Certificate (TC) & Clearance

TC Release & Dues
  • Dues Settlement: The Transfer Certificate (TC) will not be issued until all school, mess, transport, and departmental dues are fully cleared.
  • Adjustment: Any outstanding dues receivable by the school will be adjusted against the student's Caution Money, and any remaining balance must be cleared prior to TC release.
