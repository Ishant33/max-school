Active Learning Cycle

💡 How We Teach

Our Classroom. Their Curiosity. We believe the best learning happens when students are actively involved.

🔍 Step 01

ASK

We encourage children to question fearlessly, nurture innate wonder, and challenge assumptions.

🧪 Step 02

EXPLORE

We let them discover through direct hands-on experience, lab investigations, and observational projects.

💡 Step 03

UNDERSTAND

We build rock-solid conceptual foundations before advancing into complex applications.

🎯 Step 04

APPLY

We connect everyday classroom learning with real-life scenarios, practical problem solving, and community context.

🧠 Step 05

THINK

We develop logical reasoning, analytical rigor, and independent critical thinking habits.

🌟 Step 06

CREATE

We give ideas the freedom and guidance to evolve into tangible projects, inventions, and possibilities.

📅 Our Yearly Planner

A structured academic year that balances scholastic rigour with co-curricular growth — planned term by term.

Term 1 · April – September

Foundation & Assessment

New session orientation, unit tests, diagnostic evaluations, and the first co-curricular calendar of clubs and activities.

Mid-Year · October

Half-Yearly Examinations

Comprehensive assessment across scholastic subjects, followed by in-depth parent-teacher analytical meetings.

Term 2 · November – February

Application & Enrichment

Inter-school competitions, PRO Max competitive test series, science exhibitions, and annual sports events.

Year-End · March

Annual Examinations & Results

CBSE board exams for senior classes, annual scholastic evaluations, graduation ceremonies, and admissions for the new academic batch.
