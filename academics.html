Curriculum

An indigenously designed, CBSE-aligned curriculum

Our curriculum is built in-house to give personalised attention to every child's physical, intellectual, emotional and social development — not just exam results.

📚

Scholastic Core

NCERT-aligned coursework across languages, mathematics, science and social studies, mapped to CBSE guidelines.

🧠

Critical Thinking

Project-based learning and classroom discussion built to develop analysis, not just recall.

🎤

Co-Scholastic Tracks

Art, music, computer and physical education woven into the weekly timetable from the early years.

Teaching Approach

Our Pedagogy

Teachers here build classrooms where mistakes are welcome, questioning is encouraged, and every student gains a reputation as an effective learner.

💬

Discussion-Led Classrooms

High student engagement and open questioning are treated as signs of a healthy classroom, not disruption.

📈

Multiple Intelligences

Lessons are designed around the understanding that children learn differently — visually, verbally, kinesthetically and more.

🖥️

Technology-Aided Learning

Classroom management supported by IT and digital media, alongside traditional Gurukul-style mentorship.
